## Deployment Steps

### Option 1: GitHub Pages (Recommended)

1. **Upload files to GitHub**
   - Go to your repository: https://github.com/joeycost27/pti-trainer
   - Upload all 8 new HTML files
   - Commit and push changes

2. **GitHub Pages will auto-deploy**
   - Your site will be live at: https://joeycost27.github.io/pti-trainer/
   - Access the new system at: https://joeycost27.github.io/pti-trainer/pti-training-index.html

### Option 2: Replace Main Index

If you want the new system to be your main page:

1. Rename `pti-training-index.html` to `index.html`
2. Upload it (this will replace your existing index)
3. Keep all other files as-is
4. Your main URL will show the new training system

## Recommended Study Schedule

### Week 1-2: Foundation
- Daily: 1-2 hours with terminology reference
- Daily: 1 exam topic from active recall
- Every other day: 15-minute inspection practice

### Week 3-4: Intensive Practice
- Daily: 2+ tree inspections with timer (15 min each)
- Daily: Systematic checklist completion
- Daily: Fungi significance practice
- Review all exam topics

### Week 4: Final Prep
- Daily: Full 2-tree mock exams (timed)
- Review weak areas from active recall
- Practice with improved pro-forma under exam conditions
